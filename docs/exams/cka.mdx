# CKA practice questions

The CKA is a 2-hour hands-on exam in a terminal, based on Kubernetes v1.37. It has five domains:

| Domain | Weight | Questions here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Troubleshooting | 30% | 6 |
| Cluster Architecture, Installation and Configuration | 25% | 5 |
| Services and Networking | 20% | 5 |
| Workloads and Scheduling | 15% | 4 |
| Storage | 10% | 4 |

The questions are original and unofficial, not taken from the real exam. Domains and weights come from the official curriculum, as listed on [Learning paths & certifications](../roadmap.mdx). The real exam asks you to do these things, not pick them, so practice the commands in the [Hands-on labs](../labs/index.mdx).
