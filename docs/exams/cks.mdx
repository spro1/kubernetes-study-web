# CKS practice questions

The CKS is a 2-hour hands-on exam in a terminal, based on Kubernetes v1.37. You must hold the CKA first. It has six domains:

| Domain | Weight | Questions here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cluster Setup | 15% | 4 |
| Cluster Hardening | 15% | 4 |
| System Hardening | 10% | 4 |
| Minimize Microservice Vulnerabilities | 20% | 5 |
| Supply Chain Security | 20% | 5 |
| Monitoring, Logging and Runtime Security | 20% | 5 |

The questions are original and unofficial, not taken from the real exam. Domains and weights come from the official curriculum, as listed on [Learning paths & certifications](../roadmap.mdx). The real exam has you harden a live cluster, so practice in the [Hands-on labs](../labs/index.mdx).
